### 💼 Featured Projects

#### 𝒌𝖆𝒾ᥫ᭡. KAI: AI Safety Education for Young People
KAI is a child-friendly AI safety learning prototype created with Jessy Morais and Martin during the LISA x BlueDot Breaking Barriers to AI Safety Hackathon.

The project helps young people examine risks including misinformation, overconfidence, manipulation, sycophancy, cognitive offloading and excessive reliance on AI systems.

The team was recognised as a strong runner-up at the hackathon.

---

#### 🦔 PangoCare: After the Seizure
An AI-assisted welfare triage prototype designed to support the responsible assessment and handover of rescued pangolins following wildlife seizures in Nigeria.

The project is designed as a non-diagnostic decision-support system. It prioritises welfare evidence, location privacy, human review and clear limits on what the system can determine.

---

#### 🦁 WildWatch Accountability Suite
A governance prototype for assessing the risks associated with AI-assisted wildlife surveillance.

The project examines stakeholder accountability, community impact, enforcement risks, data retention, appeals, transparency and responsible human oversight.

---

#### 🌍 Multilingual AI Safety Research
Research and writing examining how language coverage affects the safety, accessibility and accountability of AI systems used in Nigeria and other multilingual environments.

This work considers model refusals, public-service access, language inequality, underrepresented communities and the limitations of evaluating African-language AI systems primarily through English-language benchmarks.

---

### 🏞 LumenAfri
A digital inclusion initiative working to expand access to digital and AI education in underserved African schools and communities.

The programme combines access to devices, practical learning resources, community partnerships and responsible AI education.
